Welcome

SPX MUN II is St. Pius X Catholic High School's Model United Nations conference, bringing together delegates for a day of debate, diplomacy, and problem-solving around this year's theme, Building Tomorrow by Solving Today. Delegates will represent nations and stakeholders across four committees, tackling issues from human rights to global health policy.

Conference Highlights

Keynote Address

Dan Reiter, professor at Emory University, will deliver the conference keynote during Opening Ceremony.

Four Committees

Delegates debate topics ranging from women's rights to the opioid crisis, child soldier rehabilitation, and a special crisis committee.

Full Day of Sessions

Four committee sessions plus opening and closing ceremonies. See the full schedule.
