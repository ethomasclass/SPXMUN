Letter from the Secretary-General

Esteemed Delegates,

It is my absolute privilege to welcome you to the first-ever St. Pius X Model United Nations Conference. My name is Stella Prévost, and for the past two years I have had the honor of serving as Secretary-General of SPXMUN.

My own journey with Model UN began my freshman year, when I walked into my first meeting without the slightest idea of what it was. By my very first committee session, I had fallen in love with the power of diplomacy. I learned how resolutions were drafted, how alliances were built, and how a single speech could shift an entire room. I stumbled, asked questions, and slowly grew more confident in my voice. Model UN pushed me out of my comfort zone and taught me skills I never expected to gain: public speaking, critical thinking, and the ability to listen as carefully as I spoke. Now, here I am, serving as your Secretary-General.

That transformation is exactly what I want for every delegate at SPXMUN. My hope is that you leave this conference with the memory of a place where you grew, learned, and connected with fellow delegates who share your passion. I want SPXMUN to be the conference where you discover your own voice, just as I discovered mine.

From the very beginning, SPXMUN has prided itself on its commitment to diplomacy and strives to create an unforgettable delegate experience that builds unity through collaboration. We aim to promote diplomacy, encourage the development of diverse skills, and challenge delegates to step out of their comfort zones.

The SPXMUN Secretariat, chairs, staff, and moderator have been working tirelessly to bring this conference to life for every delegate. All updates will be posted here on our website, and you can follow us on Instagram at @spx.model.un. On behalf of the entire SPXMUN team, thank you for making this conference possible. If you have any questions at all, please don’t hesitate to reach out to me at sprevost27@spxstudent.org or to our moderator at ethomas@spx.org.

Thank you so much for your time. We look forward to hosting you in November!

Sincerely,

Stella Prévost

Secretary-General, SPX MUN II
